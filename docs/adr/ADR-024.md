# ADR 024: GET vs two-step confirm + 2-fail DTMF

## Context
The six card actions are closed-set embeddings (`core/intents`). Callers still need a product loop: unknown numbers must not hear stub data; reads can answer once; block/unblock must not mutate on a single noisy utterance; two failed listens should fall back to keypad.

## Decision
- Implement `IntentTurnEngine` in `services/ivr/intent_turns.py`. Keep `PlaceholderTurnEngine` for FEAT-03 keyword/TTFB tests. Do not wire Twilio in this phase.
- Unknown or withheld `From`: play `unknown_caller` and hang up. No menu, no store mutation.
- GET actions (`get_balance`, `get_pin`, `get_card`, `get_card_statement`) are one-shot. Spoken PIN is the SMS placeholder only; overlay digits are never interpolated or logged.
- `block_card` / `unblock_card`: restatement with stub last-4, then yes/no. Yes calls `CallerStore.set_blocked`. Yes/no uses `ConfirmInterpreter`: a multilingual word list (`confirm_words.json`), not the six-action embedder and not a translator (one or two tokens; extra model would miss the confirm latency budget). Extra non-yes/no words other than short fillers stay unclear so DTMF can take over.
- Two consecutive listen rejects (low score or ambiguous) play `dtmf_actions` (keys 1–6). Two consecutive unclear confirms play `dtmf_confirm` (1=yes, 2=no).
- Catalog lines with `{slots}` skip phrase-cache warmup and render through fallback TTS. Static lines stay warmed.
- Logs: phrase id, action, mode, hangup — not transcript text, PIN digits, or full E.164.

## Trade-off Analysis
- **Benefit:** Dialogue stays a thin client of the router and stub store; tests inject both. Confirm does not pollute card-action prototypes.
- **Drawback:** Slot lines miss the 500 ms canned-buffer path. Hebrew/Arabic copy and live media-stream wiring wait for later phases.
